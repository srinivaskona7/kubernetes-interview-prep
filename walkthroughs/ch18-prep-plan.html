⚠ Predicted failure

  • Pitfall: Reading notes passively until the night before
  • If ignored, this surfaces as: Degraded.

✓ Best troubleshooting path

  • Reproduce with the diagnostic command (step 1-2).
  • Week 1: revisit Pods, Services, storage, scheduling and limits, and practise explaining each in thirty seconds.
  • Week 2: drill one failure symptom a day, such as Pending, crash loops and evictions, with the exact commands you would run.
  • Week 3: do mock live-debug and whiteboard rounds with a friend, record yourself, and fix the gaps.
  • Apply the fix command (step 3), then verify (step 4).
  • Confirm status becomes Healthy.

Explain

  • Week 1: revisit Pods, Services, storage, scheduling and limits, and practise explaining each in thirty seconds.
  • Week 2: drill one failure symptom a day, such as Pending, crash loops and evictions, with the exact commands you would run.
  • Week 3: do mock live-debug and whiteboard rounds with a friend, record yourself, and fix the gaps.
  • Week 4: finalise four stories, write your questions, and keep the last two days light.

Key points

  • Practise speaking, not only reading
  • Spaced daily drills beat cramming
  • Mock rounds are the highest-value week
  • Protect sleep before the day
