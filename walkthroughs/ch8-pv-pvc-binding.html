What is a PersistentVolume, and how does a Pod claim one?

Chapter 8 junior walkthrough · 4 steps
Say first

A PV is a piece of storage in the cluster that exists independently of any Pod. A Pod does not use it directly: it references a PersistentVolumeClaim, and Kubernetes binds that claim to a PV that satisfies the request.

⚠ Predicted failure

  • Pitfall: Mounting a PV directly in a Pod spec; only claims are referenced.
  • Pitfall: Forgetting that a PVC stays Pending until a matching or provisioned PV exists.
  • If ignored, this surfaces as: Pending.

✓ Best troubleshooting path

  • Reproduce with the diagnostic command (step 1-2).
  • Think of renting a storage unit. The PV is the unit itself, built by the facility (an admin, or automatically by a provisioner). The PVC is your rental request: 'I need 10 GiB, one writer at a time.' Kubernetes is the clerk who matches request to unit. Your Pod only holds the receipt (the claim name).
  • Binding checks capacity (the PV must be at least as big as asked), access mode, storage class, and optional label selectors. Once bound, the pair is exclusive: one claim, one PV.
  • Because the claim is separate from the Pod, deleting the Pod does not delete the data. A replacement Pod referencing the same claim finds the same files.
  • Apply the fix command (step 3), then verify (step 4).
  • Confirm status becomes Bound.

Explain

The PVC is your rental request: 'I need 10 GiB, one writer at a time.' Kubernetes is the clerk who matches request to unit. Your Pod only holds the receipt (the claim name).
  • Binding checks capacity (the PV must be at least as big as asked), access mode, storage class, and optional label selectors. Once bound, the pair is exclusive: one claim, one PV.
  • Because the claim is separate from the Pod, deleting the Pod does not delete the data. A replacement Pod referencing the same claim finds the same files.

Key points

  • PV is a cluster-level resource (not namespaced); PVC is namespaced.
  • Matching considers size, access mode, storageClassName and selectors.
  • A bound PVC and PV are one-to-one.
  • Pods reference the claim by name in spec.volumes.persistentVolumeClaim.
  • Pod deletion leaves the claim and data in place.
